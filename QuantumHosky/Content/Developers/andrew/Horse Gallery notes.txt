The horses are arranged in order of how biologically plausible I consider them to be, with Horse 4,4 (rectangular) being most plausible, and Horse 3,3 (alt) least.
4,4 (rectangular) and 4,4 (square) may appear similar, but the difference is that the rectangular one has lower symmetry. The rectangular one could have 2 or 4 eyes and ears, while the square one kind of has to have 4 of each. The precise distances between the legs are not meant to be important.
